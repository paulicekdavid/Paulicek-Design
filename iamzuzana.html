Iamzuzana

Zuzana is a local fitness coach for women whose clients love her for her authentic, contagious energy and the genuine joy she brings to workout. They say she can empathize with them and truly help them understand their problems. The goal was to create a brand that sets her apart from the boring and clichéd fitness competition and create something different that women could actually easily relate to. The identity conveys a sense of confidence, energy, familiarity, and enthusiasm. It doesn't have that unpleasant, intimidating, overly aggressive and pushy gym feeling that nobody likes. After I presented moodboards and my ideas how the brand could look, Zuzana immediately wanted this fresh and energetic green colour. Messaging and tone & voice are human, relatable and understanding that encourages and connects with women and speaks to them in a friendly, down-to-earth yet energetic way.

I love working with wonderful people building remarkable
things. Let’s create something that will turn heads
